 12864LCD    ESP-12
1 CS    ->  GPIO 13
2 RST   ->  GPIO 16
3 RS/DC ->  GPIO 2   (the sketch uses dc=2; GPIO 15 is not used)
4 SCK   ->  GPIO 14
5 SDA   ->  GPIO 12
6 VCC   ->  VCC
7 GND   ->  GND
8 LED-  ->  GND
9 LED+  ->  3.3V or PWM Output (backlight PWM is GPIO 0, a boot-strap pin:
             the driver must not pull it LOW at power-up or the ESP boots into flash mode)
